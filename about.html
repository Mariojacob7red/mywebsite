ABOUT ME

A bit of background, hobbies, and how I make things.

Background

I grew up in Western Arkansas in a place called Greenwood. Even though I only lived there until I was 4, I still consider it home. My family moved around a lot growing up — 10 different houses in total — before finally settling in Branson, Missouri, which we now call home.

Hobbies

I love playing video games, especially Mario games made by Nintendo. I've been making YouTube videos for the past 14 years, since April 26, 2012 — not for the money, just because I enjoy bringing a bit of happiness to anyone who finds my videos.

Back in September 2020, I became director of Horizon's Project, a gaming club on campus at SBU. I also grew up playing a lot of sports — tennis included — soccer's the only one I haven't picked up, but would love to someday!

Skills & Tools

I love using Adobe Photoshop and InDesign, and I've recently started with Inkscape. Cyberlink PowerDirector 19 is my video editor of choice — I've used it for every video on my channel.
