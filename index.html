From the shelf

A few custom pieces — box art and character work, done from scratch.

Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon

Here is my custom box art for Luigi's Mansion 2! I found the official Switch cover a bit too cluttered, so I took it upon myself to redesign it. More importantly, I wanted to honor the game's true identity. Originally titled Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon only in North America, it was known as Luigi's Mansion 2 everywhere else. It deserves to be called by its original, global name, so I replicated the artwork from scratch to restore that classic look.

Princess Peach Showtime!

This is my custom box art for Princess Peach: Showtime! While I love Nintendo's concept of showcasing Peach's various transformations, I wanted to highlight her classic, everyday smile on the cover. Using official promotional art as a reference, I recreated both the front and back covers from scratch to capture that iconic look.

Minecraft: Story Mode

In June 2024, I embarked on a self-directed learning journey in Blender to produce assets for my YouTube series, Minecraft: Story Mode. The project involved replacing standard game characters with custom 3D models based on personal and friend-derived skins. Each asset was modeled from the ground up, demonstrating a consistent trajectory of skill development and technical growth.
